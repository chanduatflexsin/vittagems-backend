How the integration works

  1. 1
    Customer pays XPZ (fiat)
    XPZ records the invoice in its own system.
  2. 2
    Settle on-chain
    POST /deposits → POST /mint → poll GET /mint/{id}. Settlement is keyed by the invoice number.
  3. 3
    Pay a partner
    POST /transfers moves the whole settlement to a partner wallet.
  4. 4
    Customer cash-out
    POST /withdrawals → XPZ pays fiat from its bank → POST /withdrawals/{id}/approve burns & closes the settlement.

Onboard XPZ

Registers XPZ as an API client (POST /clients/register) with a settlement wallet and the scopes MINT, TRANSFER, WITHDRAW, WITHDRAW_STATUS, TRANSACTION_READ. The API key is returned once and stored in this browser.
